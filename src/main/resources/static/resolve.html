Resolve

Automated conflict, oversized-berth, and missing-data resolution. Only current/future reservations are actionable here — a reservation that already happened can't be un-booked, so historical flags stay informational (see the Dashboard and Import Review) and are just counted below, not listed. There's no email/SMS hookup in this prototype: "notify" means a log entry here — you still place the call or send the email yourself using the contact info on file.
